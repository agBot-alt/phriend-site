Who holds the account

phriend is designed to be set up by an adult. Signing in uses Sign in with Apple, and the adult who signs in is the account holder. A parent or guardian sets up a phriend for a child and enters the child's year of birth so the phriend can speak at the right level.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Apple we receive an anonymous user identifier and your email address. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address — we never see your real one.

Your phriend. Its name, species, appearance seed, personality, growth stage, and your settings: alarm, get-ready routine, nudge frequency, quiet and sleep hours, and your device's timezone.

Year of birth (children only). When a phriend is set up for a child we store the year only — not a full date of birth. It is used to pitch vocabulary appropriately and to apply the child-safety rules below, and it is the minimum that achieves both.

Your conversations. What you say to your phriend and what it says back. These are automatically deleted after 30 days.

Things your phriend learns. For adult accounts, we derive short interest tags from conversations (for example "dinosaurs") so your phriend has something worth talking about. We do not do this for children's accounts — a child's conversations are never mined for interests.

Where you are — as a place name only. Your phone works out the town or city you are in and, sometimes, a general venue type such as "cinema" or "airport". Only that word is sent to us. Your exact coordinates never leave your phone, and our servers actively reject any request that contains them.

We do not keep a history of where you have been. Only your most recent reading is stored, and each new one replaces the last. Separately, a few notable moments — arriving somewhere new, going on a trip — are kept as a short note for 90 days so your phriend can refer back to them.

We deliberately do not detect sensitive places. The app never looks for hospitals, pharmacies, clinics or places of worship, so it cannot know you were at one.

Weather. The conditions and temperature where you are, so your phriend can talk about the rain.

Movement — as a single word. If you allow it, your phone sends one word: "stationary", "walking", "running", "driving" or "cycling". No route, no journey, no location.

Focus. Whether your phone is in a Focus mode, so your phriend knows to wait. We can only see that a Focus is on, never which one.

Your device's notification token, so we can send your phriend's messages.

We do not collect: your exact location, your calendar, your contacts, your photos, your health data, or your advertising identifier.

What we do not do

  • No advertising. No ads, no ad networks, no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing your data. Ever, to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. There are none in the app.
  • No profiling for marketing.

Why we are allowed to hold it

We process this data to provide the app you asked for — that is, to perform our agreement with you. Where we rely on your permission (location, motion, Focus, notifications), you grant it in the phone's own permission prompts and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings; the phriend keeps working without it, it just notices less.

Children

phriend is built to be safe for children.

  • Every reply a child sees passes two checks — the phriend is instructed to keep language age-appropriate, and a second, separate check runs over the reply before it is shown. If either is unhappy, the child sees a safe standard message instead of the generated one.
  • A child's conversations are never mined for interests.
  • Nudges are silenced overnight and during school hours by default.
  • If a child tells their phriend something upsetting, the phriend is instructed to respond warmly, never to promise secrecy, never to give advice, and to encourage them to talk to a grown-up they trust — and to mention Childline (0800 1111) where that is the right thing to do. The phriend is a companion, not a counsellor, and it does not treat, diagnose or advise on any condition.
  • We do not tell parents what a child confides. Auto-reporting would break the one channel a child might have. What a parent can always see is everything the phriend has learned — see below.

Seeing and deleting your data

To see it: the app has a screen showing everything the phriend knows about you — what it has learned, your interests, what it has noticed, and what it has looked up. It is read directly from our database, so it is the real thing, not a summary.

To delete one thing: swipe it away on that screen.

To delete everything: send your phriend off. This permanently deletes their profile, conversations, interests, everything they noticed and everything they looked up. It cannot be undone.

We keep one thing afterwards: a short memorial record — the phriend's name, species, dates, final stage and where they went — so you can look back at the phriends you have had. It contains nothing you told them.

Your rights. Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, object to how we use it, or ask for it in a portable form. Contact us at the address above and we will respond within one month.
