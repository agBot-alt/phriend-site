Support

Reporting a problem

The quickest route is from inside the app: open About and tap Send feedback. It attaches the app version and a few diagnostics automatically, which usually saves a round of back-and-forth working out what your phriend could and couldn't see.

A grown-up needs to complete a short check before it opens your email app — that is deliberate, so a child can't send mail on their own.

If you'd rather write directly, it helps to include:

  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • Your phriend's name, and roughly when it happened
  • The app version, shown at the top of the About screen

Something your phriend said

Your phriend's replies are generated, and every reply shown to a child passes an additional safety check before it appears. Checks are not perfect. If your phriend says anything upsetting, wrong or inappropriate, please tell us — email support@phriend.uk with roughly what was said and when, and we will look into it.

Reports about a child's safety are read first.

Ideas — "could phriend do this?"

Yes, please send these. phriend is early and the list of things it can do is deliberately short, so ideas genuinely shape what gets built next. There is no wrong suggestion and nothing is too small — "it should notice when I'm on a train" is as useful as anything grander.

The most useful thing you can tell us is the moment you wanted it, rather than a description of the feature. "I got home late and wished it had already known" tells us far more than "add a home detection setting", because it says what you were doing and what you expected — and there is usually a better answer than the one either of us would have thought of first.

Also worth sending:

  • Things your phriend does that you'd like more of — those are as useful as complaints, and much rarer
  • Anything it does that feels off, annoying or too chatty, even if nothing is broken
  • What your child does with it that surprised you

We read everything. We can't promise to build it, and we'd rather say that plainly than imply a roadmap we don't have — but ideas are where most of the good ones have come from so far.

Your data

To see everything your phriend knows about you, open it in the app — there's a screen listing what it has learned, your interests, what it has noticed and what it has looked up, read straight from our database. You can delete individual items there by swiping.

To delete everything, send your phriend off from inside the app. That permanently removes their profile, conversations, interests and everything they noticed.

You can also ask us directly for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Email us and we will respond within one month.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

A note for parents

phriend is meant to be set up by an adult. When a phriend is created for a child, its language is pitched to their age, its replies are checked before being shown, and it stays quiet overnight and during school hours by default.

Your phriend is a companion, not a counsellor. If a child tells it something upsetting it responds warmly and encourages them to talk to a grown-up they trust, and it will mention Childline where that is the right thing to do. It does not treat, diagnose or give advice on any condition, and it never reports what a child says back to you — what you can always see is everything it has learned, on the screen described above.

If a child needs to talk to someone in the UK:
Childline — 0800 1111, free, any time.
Samaritans — 116 123, free, any time, any age.
