phriend

A little friend who grows with you

phriend lives behind the glass of your iPhone. It notices the weather where you are, remembers what you like, and grows up over time. Every phriend looks and behaves a little differently, and its look is its own for life.

phriend is not on the App Store yet. This page exists so you can reach us and read how your data is handled before it is.

It is made by one person in their spare time, which is worth knowing before you write in — replies are slow, but they do come.

The short version on privacy

  • Your exact location never leaves your phone. Your phriend only ever learns the name of the town you're in.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. None, anywhere in the app.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • Conversations are deleted after 30 days, and you can delete everything yourself, permanently, at any time.
  • You can see exactly what your phriend knows about you on a screen in the app, read straight from the database.

The whole thing is in the Privacy Policy.

Will phriend always be free?

It is free today, and we would like to keep it that way for as long as we can.

Being straight with you about why that might change: every conversation your phriend has costs real money to run, and that cost grows with the number of people using it. phriend is made by one person, not a company with funding to burn.

If it grows enough that the running costs stop being affordable, something will have to give — most likely a charge, or the option of an ad-supported version alongside a free one. We do not know yet, and we would rather say so than promise something we might not be able to keep.

Two things will not change, whichever way it goes:

  • We will never sell your data. Not to anyone, for any purpose.
  • There will never be behavioural advertising to a child — advertising that follows them around based on what they do. The App Store forbids it for apps built for children, and we would not want it.

And we will tell you before anything changes, not after. Your phriend is never held to ransom either: whatever happens, you can always see everything it knows about you, and take it away or delete it.
