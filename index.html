A little friend who grows with you

phriend lives behind the glass of your iPhone. It notices the weather where you are, remembers what you like, and grows up over time. Every phriend looks and behaves a little differently, and its look is its own for life.

phriend is not on the App Store yet. This page exists so you can reach us and read how your data is handled before it is.

The short version on privacy

  • Your exact location never leaves your phone. Your phriend only ever learns the name of the town you're in.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. None, anywhere in the app.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • Conversations are deleted after 30 days, and you can delete everything yourself, permanently, at any time.
  • You can see exactly what your phriend knows about you on a screen in the app, read straight from the database.

The whole thing is in the Privacy Policy.
